Chunking strategy notes.

Chunk size is the single most consequential RAG parameter. Chunks that are too small lose the
context needed to answer, while chunks that are too large dilute the embedding and retrieve
irrelevant text alongside the relevant part.

A token-based splitter is preferable to a character-based one because the embedding model's
context limit is expressed in tokens, not characters.

Overlap between consecutive chunks prevents an answer from being split across a boundary.

Metadata attached to a chunk is not embedded. It is stored next to the vector and can be used to
narrow the similarity search with a filter expression, which is far cheaper than retrieving
everything and filtering afterwards.
